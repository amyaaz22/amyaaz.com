Questions

Before you write.

Which Zoho products do you work with?

Zoho Creator is the core, with Deluge scripting, Zoho Flow automations and Catalyst serverless functions around it. I also connect Creator to Zoho Books and CRM.

Can you extend a Zoho setup we already have?

Yes. I start by reading what exists, then extend it rather than rebuilding what already works.

Do you work outside Mauritius?

Yes. Projects are scoped, built and handed over online, mainly for teams in the Middle East and Africa. Mauritius runs on UTC+4, the same as the UAE.

Can you handle MRA e-invoicing?

Yes. I build it into the invoice flow so invoices submit as part of normal work.

How does a project start?

Send a short note about the problem. I reply with questions and a proposed scope.
